Good to know

Questions

Are the apps free?

Yes, all four are free to download. Each also has an optional Pro upgrade that unlocks extra features. There are no ads in any of them.

When can I download them?

The apps are coming to Google Play for Android. An iPhone version of Solar is planned.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and it works. There is nothing to sign up for.

Do the apps collect my data?

No. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your location is used on your phone to work out what is above you and is never sent to us. See the privacy policy.

Where does the data come from?

From public science agencies: NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center, NASA, NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory, CelesTrak, Breakthrough Listen and Open-Meteo.

Do the apps work offline?

Each app keeps the latest data it downloaded and shows it when you are offline, so you can still check the last forecast or pass times.

Questions or ideas?

Found a bug, want a feature, or spotted something odd in the sky data? Get in touch.
